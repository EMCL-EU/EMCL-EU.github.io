# Invitation

As you may know, the EMCL programme is celebrating its silver anniversary! We are celebrating this milestone in Groningen. 

WHO: friends, students, alumni, staff of the EMCL;

WHAT: Introduction lecture, speakers, and alumni reunion;

WHEN: 
- Sunday, May 25, afternoon celebration with drinks;
- Monday, May 26, 9 am-12.30 pm we will be hosting a morning of talks on the past, present and future of the EMCL;
- Monday, May 26, 2 - 4 pm alumni reunion;

WHERE: 
- (In person) Monday-morning: Senaatszaal, Academy Building, Broerstraat, Groningen, afternoon in the Harmonie Building for alumni reunion;
- (Online) Video call link will be shared on request. If you are unable to attend in person in Groningen, you can participate online.
 
Please let us know if you would like to attend and participate with us in celebrating EMCL's silver anniversary as soon as possible, but ultimately by May 6, 2025 via [emcl@rug.nl](mailto:emcl@rug.nl).
