Modules

© UG, photographer: Peter Tahl

All modules of the EMCL-25 programme are graded with ECTS. A student has to obtain at least 120 ECTS, 30 ECTS in each term, including internship and thesis. The programme is divided into 7 modules:

Module 1: Linguistics (8 ECTS)
  • Language and Languages - A Course on General Linguistics (UEF, 1st term, 5 ECTS)
  • Language and Languages - Linguistic Reading Group (UEF, 1st term, 3 ECTS)
Module 2: Methods and Statistics (24 ECTS)
  • Empirical Research Methods in Linguistics (UEF, 1st term, 5 ECTS)
  • Introduction to Python Programming for Linguistic Research (UEF, 1st term, 3 ECTS)
  • Introduction to Phonetics and the Praat Tool (UEF, 1st term, 2 ECTS)
  • Statistics and Data Visualisation for Linguistics (UEF, 1st term, 5 ECTS)
  • Introduction to Praat Script Programming for Clinical Linguistic Research(UEF, 1st term, 2 ECTS)
  • Statistics and Research Methods Applied to the Development and Validation of Language Research Tools (UGENT, 2nd term, 7 ECTS)
Module 3: Psycholinguistics (11 ECTS)
  • Bilingualism (UGent, 2nd term, 4 ECTS)
  • Computational Models in Psycholinguistics (UGent, 2nd term, 4 ECTS)
  • Advances in Psycholinguistics (UGent, 2nd term, 3 ECTS)
Module 4: Clinical Linguistics and Neurolinguistics (19 ECTS)
  • Introduction to Developmental and Acquired Speech and Language Problems (UEF/RUG, 1st term, 5 ECTS)
  • Reading Acquisition and Dyslexia (UGent, 2nd term, 4 ECTS)
  • Advanced course in Developmental and Acquired Speech and Language Problems (RUG, 3rd term, 5 ECTS)
  • Language Testing in Awake Brain Surgery (RUG, 3rd term, 5 ECTS)
Module 5: Neurotechnology and IT for clinical linguistics (13 ECTS)
  • Eye-tracking in Language Research (UGent, 2nd term, 3 ECTS)
  • Apps and Games in Clinical Linguistics (RUG, 3rd term, 5 ECTS)
  • Neuroimaging and Language (RUG, 3rd term, 5 ECTS)
Module 6: Internship (15 ECTS)
  • Internship Preparation (UGent, 2nd term, 5 ECTS)
  • Internship will take place in the 3rd term and will be conducted at one of the associated partner institutions. (3rd term, 10 ECTS)
Module 7: Thesis (30 ECTS)
The thesis will be written in the fourth term, under supervision of (at least) a teacher at one of the three partner universities. (4th term, 30 ECTS)

For a list of courses per term, please check the course overview. More detailed descriptions of the courses can be found in this document.
