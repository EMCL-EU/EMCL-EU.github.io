Application/admission

What is the name of the European Master in Clinical Linguistics (EMCL) in Studielink? I cannot find it!
The name of the EMCL programme in "Studielink" is "Research Master in Clinical Linguistics".
I have not received my diploma yet/I will finish my BA in 2026. Is that a problem?
Not a problem at all. You need to send us (so upload it in the application portal) a transcript of records plus a statement of your university that you will finish your studies before the 1st of September 2026.
What are the requirements for the motivation letter?
The motivation letter should be 1-2 pages. Not longer than 2 pages in any case.

Programme

When does the EMCL programme start?
The programme starts on September 1, 2026.
Which semester is offered by which partner/where?
Starting with the cohort 2025-2027, the first semester will be spent at UEF (Finland), the second semester at UGENT (Belgium) and the third semester at RUG (the Netherlands). The location of the final semester depends on the internship and thesis and could be at either of the partner's institutions or at a different location (external partner).
